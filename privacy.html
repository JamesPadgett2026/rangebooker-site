Privacy Policy

This policy covers information Tropical Tech uses to provide its SMS program.

Information we use

When you opt in, we use your mobile number and a record of when and how you agreed to receive texts. We use message replies and delivery information to provide the messages you requested, answer questions and honor opt-outs.

How we share information

We do not sell mobile numbers or share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in information or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging providers, including Twilio and wireless carriers, process information as needed to deliver texts and handle replies.

Your choices

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. For questions about the use of your mobile number, email jpadgett@tropicaltech.com.
